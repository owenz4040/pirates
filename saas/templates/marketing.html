Zero-Inbound Attack Surface

Pull-Based Architecture Keeps Your Routers 100% Secure

Traditional billing platforms require exposing your MikroTik API port (`8728`) or SSH to the public internet, leaving your routers vulnerable to brute-force attacks.

COwen uses an authenticated pull-sync agent: your MikroTik fetches pending subscriber queues and captive portal updates over TLS HTTPS once every 60 seconds.

✔ No Public IP Needed
✔ No Port Forwarding
✔ Works Behind CGNAT
# 1. Enrolling your router into COwen:
/system scheduler add name="cloud_sync" interval=60s on-event={
  /tool fetch url="https://cowen.app/api/router/sync" \
    http-header-field="Authorization: Bearer <TOKEN_HASH>" \
    dst-path="cloud_sync.rsc"
  /import cloud_sync.rsc
}

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a static public IP on my MikroTik?
No. Because COwen uses authenticated pull synchronization over HTTPS, your router can be behind Starlink, standard Safaricom/Airtel SIM routers, or carrier-grade NAT (CGNAT) without any special IP configurations.
Where do customer M-Pesa payments go?
Directly into your own business bank or till/paybill account. You configure your own Safaricom Daraja Consumer Key, Secret, and Passkey in your tenant settings. COwen never touches your subscriber funds.
How does subscription billing for COwen work?
We bill a flat monthly subscription via Paystack based on the number of MikroTik routers you manage (e.g. KSh 1,000 for 1 router up to KSh 5,000 for 6 routers). You can upgrade or cancel anytime.
Can I build customized captive portals with my own brand?
Yes! Our visual hotspot builder lets you customize brand colors, logos, pricing tiers, and announcement messages, and deploys the files directly to your router with one click.
